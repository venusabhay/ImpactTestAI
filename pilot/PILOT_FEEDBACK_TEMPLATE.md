## Questions (answer in your own words — a sentence or two each is fine)

1. **Did this tell us something useful?** What, specifically?

2. **Did it change what we would have tested?** If yes, how — more, less, or different validation than your normal process?

3. **Did it save us time?** Rough sense is fine — no need for a stopwatch.

4. **Did it identify something our normal process missed?** If yes, what was it, and how serious would it have been if it reached production?

5. **Did it ask us to do unnecessary work?** What made it feel unnecessary — was the risk assessment wrong, or was the recommendation just not worth the effort for the actual risk?

6. **Did we trust the explanation?** When it said something was risky (or not), could you tell *why* from the report, or did it feel like a black box?

7. **Would we use this again?** Yes / No / Maybe, and why.

## Anything else

Anything that surprised you, confused you, or that you think we should know — including if the tool's assumptions about your repository's structure didn't match reality (see "Important things to know" in [PILOT.md](../PILOT.md)). We want the honest result, including "this didn't help" or "this got it wrong" — that's exactly what this pilot is for.
